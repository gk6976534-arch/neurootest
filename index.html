The official NeuroTest® product page lists these seven ingredients:

Also known as tongkat ali, this standardized botanical extract is included in NeuroTest to support men's vitality and healthy testosterone levels. Research on tongkat ali is ongoing, and results depend on the extract and dosage.

Krachaidum (Black Ginger)

Black ginger is a traditional botanical used in men's wellness supplements. It contains plant compounds called methoxyflavones and is included in the formula to support vitality and physical wellness.
3

Mucuna Pruriens

Mucuna pruriens is a tropical plant whose seeds naturally contain L-DOPA. It has been studied for its effects on dopamine-related pathways, but it can interact with medications and is not suitable for everyone.
4

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

Ashwagandha is an herb traditionally used in Ayurvedic wellness practices. Some research has examined its potential effects on stress and men's reproductive health, although evidence remains limited and varies by preparation.
5

Bacopa

Bacopa is a botanical traditionally associated with cognitive wellness. It has been studied for memory and cognitive performance, but these potential effects do not establish a testosterone-related benefit.
6

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo biloba is a plant extract used in various wellness supplements. It has been studied for circulation and other health outcomes, but evidence for specific men's vitality benefits is limited. It may interact with blood-thinning medication.
7

Phosphatidylserine

Phosphatidylserine is a naturally occurring phospholipid found in cell membranes. It is included in some supplements for cognitive and cellular support, although its role in testosterone support is not established.
